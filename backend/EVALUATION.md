# Evaluation Procedure

Use synthetic fixtures with manually reviewed expected events. For parser accuracy, compare normalized severity, timestamp, category, and source line against labeled records and calculate precision/recall per field. For retrieval, prepare a small set of permitted, version-tagged documents and queries; report whether a supporting source occurs in the top 3 and manually verify title, URL, publisher, and product compatibility. For diagnostics, have an engineer label observations versus hypotheses and score evidence-reference validity separately from diagnostic usefulness. For actions, review risk category, prerequisites, reversibility, and whether the cited record supports the stated reason. Track engineer feedback as feedback, never as verified ground truth. Do not evaluate on confidential production logs without approved controls.
